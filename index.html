Source and data-quality notes

Update frequency: Melbourne player and team stats are updated shortly after each game's statistics are uploaded and processed. Opponent stats refresh only when a new advance scouting report for that club is published, usually before Melbourne next faces them. There is no live GameDay synchronization.

Melbourne: eight-game cumulative report is the source for player and team statistics. The official ladder screenshot lists Melbourne in 8th place (4–4, 52 for, 57 against). Batting AVG qualification is 2 PA per team game. Melbourne OBP and OPS in the sortable tables are estimated using (H + BB) / (AB + BB) and estimated OBP + SLG (using unrounded values); historical HBP, SF and CI are not completely recorded. Values may differ from official OBP/OPS. Pitching appearance counts remain unverified. Game 8 batting BB/SO and Perrett's H/BB/SO/ER are provisional interpretations of an unheaded GameDay player-stat table; verify against the headed box score.

Opponents: Doncaster five games, Upwey FTG four games, Sandringham six games, Waverley five official games with incomplete player pitching records; Moorabbin six official games with five supplied player-stat batches (November 10 player stats still missing). A missing stat in an opponent report is not a zero. The team total rows are displayed separately, not treated as players or summed across clubs.

Classification: THREAT/PRIORITY (red), CAUTION (yellow), ATTACK (green), LIMITED (gray), following each source. The source reports sometimes use different qualification conventions.

Pitching source discrepancy: the new game's pitcher-run fields sum to eight, but the verified final score was 21–4. Official team runs allowed follow the score; pitcher component statistics are provisional pending box-score confirmation.

Reports: all previous PDFs remain available, plus the new eight-game report and extracted plain-text copies. The weekly Melbourne Round 5 report is archival and is not added to the later six-game totals, to avoid double counting.

Structured source: league_stats.json. Historical snapshot, not live results.
