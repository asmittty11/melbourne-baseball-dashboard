Sources and limitations

This dashboard is a static, browser-based conversion of the supplied Melbourne Shiny package. It does not fetch live league scores or silently update player statistics.

Melbourne player and team statistics are available as the supplied season PDFs. Sandringham's interactive tables use the provided five-game cumulative CSVs. Doncaster and Upwey scouting is available in the supplied PDF reports. Historical box scores and player appearances have not been fully reconciled.

To update the site, replace the appropriate data and reports and republish. The JSON source is included at data/dashboard.json.
