Source And Data-Quality Notes

Update Frequency: Melbourne player and team stats are updated shortly after each game's statistics are uploaded and processed. Opponent stats refresh only when a new advance scouting report for that club is published, usually before Melbourne next faces them. There is no live GameDay synchronization.

Melbourne: the official November 24 ladder shows Melbourne 9th (5–6–1, 67 for, 89 against) through twelve games. Player statistics are available for only eleven: the November 21 Sunshine 18–5 loss added nine hits, two homers, five runs scored and 8.0 pitching innings (18 R, 11 ER); the November 24 9–8 road victory against Malvern is standings-only until player box scores arrive. Batting AVG qualification is 24 PA; pitching ERA qualification is 12 outs (4.0 IP). OBP/OPS remain estimates because historic HBP, SF, CI are incomplete. Earlier Game 8 Perrett pitching remains provisional; Zak Skinner and Lennard Pietersz sacrifice-bunt/PA conflicts are flagged. The nine team stolen bases are the verified minimum, not necessarily a complete season total; player G counts are not verified.

Opponents: Official November 24 standings: Waverley 1st (9–0–0, 95–14), Moorabbin 3rd (7–1–1, 71–36), Malvern 4th (8–3–0, 79–46), Sunshine 5th (7–4–0, 84–37). Malvern's uploaded individual statistics remain at nine earlier supplied game batches while the official record covers eleven games (including the new 9–8 loss to Melbourne). Its older pitching ledger had 28 pitcher runs versus 23 official runs allowed in the nine-game snapshot; verify that historical discrepancy. Zaid Elias Cummins Flynn's pitching read remains STRONG per approved override. Sunshine still has only six usable player game batches from eleven official games; the November 21 18–5 win over Melbourne is in the ladder but its Sunshine individual player lines have not been supplied. One older malformed batch remains excluded. Moorabbin's individual sample remains incomplete. Other opponents retain historical samples; missing stats are never filled with zeros, and team totals are not ranked as players.

Classification: batting THREAT (red), pitching STRONG (red), CAUTION (yellow), ATTACK (green), and LIMITED (gray), following each source. The source reports sometimes use different qualification conventions.

Pitching Source Discrepancies: the November 21 Sunshine loss has 18 runs allowed and 11 earned across six Melbourne pitchers, matching the final runs allowed; seven are unearned under the supplied scoring. Earlier pitching component discrepancies, including prior source entries for Sandringham, remain documented rather than silently corrected. Missing November 24 Malvern pitching lines are not treated as zero.

Reports: Original historical PDFs remain archived. The new eleven-player-game Melbourne PDF (with twelve-game official standings) and the Sunshine/Malvern scouting previews are listed alongside previous reports and searchable text. The earlier Round 5 weekly Melbourne report is archival and is not added again to player totals.
